# Whole-circuit distillation: seed-0 development result

Status: **development evidence only**. Validation was report-only and the
official test split remained sealed.

## What was implemented

- Joint GPU optimization of all 32,000 eligible internal LUTs over all 16
  rank-2 Boolean functions, starting from the original hardened checkpoint.
- Winner--runner decision-margin, worst-class, stratified-fold, label, and
  hardware-proxy losses.
- A stratified 75%/25% optimization/repair split inside the calibration
  partition.
- Deterministic hardening and exact full-repair-subset checks of global and
  per-class accuracy/disagreement budgets.
- Saved soft optimizer state, hard model, LUT changes, training trace, repair
  trace, sample hashes, software versions, circuits, compiled-C equivalence,
  and identical Yosys/ABC synthesis.

No Unit-Tying checkpoint, shortlist, Binary Split, warm start, or fixed rewrite
quota is used by the primary method.

## Results

| Method | Val. acc. | Val. disagreement | Live gates | ABC nodes | Levels |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Original exact-simplified | 87.300% | 0% | 33,843 | 99,365 | 78 |
| Two-Stage Unit Tying, 10% | 86.967% | 2.483% | 30,405 | 94,084 | 79 |
| Full actions, AIG proxy | 87.233% | 0.767% | 32,665 | 96,740 | 78 |
| Full actions, gate-count proxy | 87.233% | 0.767% | 31,440 | 96,519 | 78 |
| Constants only | 87.250% | 1.900% | 32,018 | 95,173 | 79 |
| MSE instead of margins | 86.967% | 1.833% | 33,183 | 97,961 | 79 |
| No group robustness | 87.100% | 1.117% | 32,681 | 96,719 | 78 |
| No exact repair (infeasible) | 85.950% | 5.167% | 27,315 | 87,898 | 76 |

The gate-count full-action point retains 2,694 learned changes, including 1,622
nonconstant changes. Thus the implementation is materially broader than
constant tying. However, it does not beat Unit Tying in ABC nodes. The
constants-only ablation is also better in ABC nodes than either full-action
point, showing that the current nonconstant action ranking improves live-gate
count but not mapped AIG cost.

Exact repair is necessary: removing it produces the only result below Unit
Tying in hardware, but that point violates the calibration constraints and
loses 1.35 percentage points of validation accuracy from the original.

## Advancement decision

The central full-action method had to remain at least as accurate as 10% Unit
Tying, contain at least 25% nonconstant changes, satisfy all calibration
budgets, and reduce ABC nodes by at least 1% beyond Unit Tying. It passes the
accuracy, novelty-action, and feasibility checks but fails the hardware check
(96,519 versus a target of at most 93,143).

Consequently:

- the five-seed study is not launched;
- the second dataset is not launched because it depends on a successful
  central multi-seed result; and
- the next methodological problem is a differentiable cost/ranking model that
  captures downstream constant propagation and ABC sharing for nonconstant
  LUT changes.

The machine-readable decision is generated by
[`evaluate_circuit_distillation_gate.py`](evaluate_circuit_distillation_gate.py).
