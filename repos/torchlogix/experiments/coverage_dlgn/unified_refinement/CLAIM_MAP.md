UR1 supplies interventions and replication, not an architecture-specific menu of
new winners. Do not replace the historical method or turn an exploratory result

## Unified presentation

1. Define one rank-two fixed-wiring constructor: explicit semantic source metadata,
   balanced multiscale matching stages and optional measured ancestry-novelty
   selection. Identify the frozen U2 setting exactly; nominal is its control.
2. Explain the common object being paired: predecessor signals in dense layers
   and channels in convolutional blocks. Preserve the convolutional spatial
   sampler and exact model budgets. Do not equate native dense random with the
   grouped convolutional native baseline.
3. Separate construction guarantees, descriptive circuit diagnostics, controlled
   interventions, training compatibility and external evaluation. None alone
   proves the others. Keep negative results visible.
4. Use matched random/U2 as the cross-architecture headline comparison. If the
   factorial does not explain the effect, narrow the mechanism claim rather than
   changing nulls or selecting whichever contrast is positive.

Recommended figures after completion: one method diagram, one matched-accuracy
table, one five-contrast factorial plot, the existing functional-support diagnostic,
and a full-S paired replication plot when its evaluation protocol completes.
The construction microbenchmark belongs in implementation details unless a
separately measured end-to-end construction benefit justifies greater emphasis.

Stages 3B (long nominal) and 4 (crossed seeds) are not queued automatically.
Their value should be judged only after the declared factorial is complete;
full-S replication proceeds independently of their outcome. Hardware stays
deferred, as requested.
