# Conclusion

In this paper, we presented LogicConnect for designing fixed DLGN connectivity through semantic input pairing and multiscale matching. The method supports dense and convolutional architectures while preserving their logical budgets. Across three dense CIFAR-10 sizes, it improves test accuracy by 3.18–4.59 percentage points over random connections. Compared with learned Top-32 routing, it reduces peak GPU memory by 93.7–94.2%, at an accuracy cost of 1.84–5.30 points. Ablations identify first-layer pairing as the strongest measured dense contribution, while the benefit of deeper structure and ancestry selection remains unresolved. Further convolutional replications and physical implementation measurements are needed to establish the consistency of the convolutional gains and their circuit-level implications.
