                                                                   We also estimate the equivalent latency and energy
                                                                of our macro in a 16 nm process for the purpose of
                                                                comparison with other works. To do so, we employ the
                                                                fan-out-of-four (FO4) inverter delay as a technology-
                                                                independent measure of logical depth [10]. The simulated
                                                                FO4 delay for 130 nm bulk CMOS is 34.7 ps and for
                                                                16 nm high-performance multi-gate (FinFET) is 6.12 ps at
                                                                their respective nominal supply voltages [10]. Our 23.9 ns
                                                                critical path thus corresponds to a logical depth of ap-
                                                                proximately 689 FO4 inverter delays. Applying the 16 nm
                                                                FO4 delay, we estimate a scaled latency of approximately
                                                                4.2 ns. For energy scaling, we apply the per-inverter energy
                                                                ratio from [10], where the simulated FO4 inverter energy
                                                                is 5.20 fJ at 130 nm and 0.179 fJ at 16 nm, yielding a
                                                                scaling factor of 0.034. Applying this to our measured
                                                                energy per inference gives an estimated 16 nm energy of
                                                                approximately 69 pJ.
